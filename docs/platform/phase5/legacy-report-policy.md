# Legacy Diagnostic Report Policy

Historical hand-authored Markdown, chat-derived summaries and acceptance prose are presentation artifacts, not Phase 5 authority. They may be linked for operator context but cannot be replayed, evaluated or migrated into canonical fields unless incident, investigation, run, target, evidence, conclusion and component lineage are reconstructed from immutable versioned sources without inference.

When lineage is reconstructable, migration creates a new canonical report with explicit source digests and gaps for every unavailable field. When it is not, the artifact remains labeled `LEGACY_NON_AUTHORITATIVE`; its prose, confidence wording and status must not influence a canonical conclusion or Judge outcome.
